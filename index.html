I am Naren Chamy, a Pre-Business student at the University of California, Riverside. My interests connect business, service, and teamwork, and my experiences have given me different ways to explore those connections. Through e-commerce and operations work with Priyum Products, volleyball coaching with SoCal Elite Volleyball, and leadership and teaching through community service, I am developing a practical foundation for my professional future. I want to understand how organizations serve people, coordinate everyday responsibilities, and turn ideas into useful results. My education at UC Riverside gives me an opportunity to connect these interests with a broader understanding of business.

My experience with Priyum Products has introduced me to the relationship between e-commerce and operations. Online business involves more than presenting a product; it also depends on the work that supports the customer experience. This perspective encourages me to think about how communication, organization, and attention to detail fit together. I am interested in learning how businesses make their processes more dependable while remaining responsive to changing needs. As I continue developing my business knowledge, I hope to connect lessons from this experience with classroom discussions about management, customers, and the decisions that shape an organization over time.

Coaching with SoCal Elite Volleyball offers another perspective on working with people. A team brings together individuals with different strengths, learning styles, and levels of confidence. Coaching encourages me to think carefully about how I communicate and how I support someone who is learning. I value the balance between individual development and shared goals, because both matter to a team's progress. Volleyball also provides a setting for practicing patience, preparation, and accountability. These are qualities I want to carry into future professional settings, where collaboration and clear expectations can help people contribute more effectively to a common purpose.

My involvement in Boy Scouts and my experience as an Eagle Scout are also important parts of my background. Leadership and service have encouraged me to consider how my actions affect the people around me. I see leadership as a responsibility to contribute, listen, and follow through. Volunteer teaching complements that perspective by creating opportunities to help others learn. It reminds me that an explanation becomes useful when it meets the learner's needs. Together, these experiences shape the way I approach teamwork: with respect for others, a willingness to participate, and an interest in helping a group move forward.

Looking ahead, I want to keep building on the connections among business, coaching, leadership, and education. I am interested in opportunities that let me learn from experienced people while contributing my own effort and perspective. My goal is to develop stronger analytical and communication skills, understand different business functions, and become a dependable colleague. This portfolio brings together the experiences that inform those goals and provides space to document future work. As my education and experience grow, I plan to update it with projects and reflections that show what I am learning and how I apply that learning. I welcome opportunities to learn, contribute, and grow.
